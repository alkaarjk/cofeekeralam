What is Coffee Keralam?

Coffee Keralam is a Kerala-inspired coffee brand website featuring coffee varieties, menu information, coffee knowledge and online ordering features.

What types of coffee are available?

Our sample menu features cappuccino, cold coffee, espresso, cafe latte, mocha and special coffee.

What is the difference between espresso and cappuccino?

Espresso is a concentrated coffee brewed under pressure. Cappuccino combines espresso with steamed milk and milk foam.
